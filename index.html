Mapping Capital Flows in the DC Region

Is capital investment distributed evenly across the Washington DC region, or does it track demographic lines?

Built an R/Quarto pipeline over Urban Institute capital-flows data to test whether county population predicts poverty rates, and how investment per household varies across racial demographic groups in the DC metro area.

Finding: larger, more urbanized counties tend to show lower poverty rates, though population size alone explains little of the variation — investment per household differs meaningfully by demographic group.

Chicago Homicide Geography

Where do homicides concentrate in Chicago, and does the arrest rate hold steady across neighborhoods?

Joined 8M+ Chicago Police Department incident records with census tract boundaries and ACS data to map homicide concentration, its relationship to income, and preliminary arrest-rate variation by neighborhood.

Finding: homicides cluster heavily on the South and West Sides; tract-level income correlates negatively with homicide rates; arrest-rate variation across neighborhoods needs further work.

Data: Chicago PD · US Census ACS Stack: sf · tidycensus Repository →
